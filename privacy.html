Word Verdict privacy

Word Verdict (aidehq.dev/wordverdict) is a small internal tool the AIDE team uses to rate words. This page says what it keeps.

What we get from Google sign-in

Only your email address, to know who you are and to let in only invited people. We do not get your password, contacts, files or anything else from your Google account.

What we keep

Your email address, and each choice you make in the app (which word, Bad / Neutral / Good or which of two words you prefer, and when). Everyone signed in to Word Verdict can see everyone's choices, with their email address. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in.

Where it is kept

On AIDE's own server. It is not sold or shared with anyone, and there are no ads or tracking.

Deleting your data

Email dor@aidehq.dev and we delete your choices and your sign-in.
